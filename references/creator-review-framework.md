# Creator Review Framework

## Review record

For every important creator capture:
- creator;
- platform;
- publication date/time;
- content title;
- hands-on vs full review vs camera comparison;
- models tested;
- duration of access if known;
- software/build if relevant;
- key imaging observations;
- performance/battery/charging/ergonomic observations that change the overall interpretation;
- limitations;
- original URL.

## Summary pattern

Write 2–5 sentences, not “Creator X posted a review.”

1. What the creator actually tested.
2. Their main conclusion.
3. The strongest imaging-specific evidence.
4. A non-imaging factor that materially changes the product story.
5. Caveat or test condition if needed.

## Third-party interpretation

Third-party analysis can add value by:
- explaining a technical phenomenon;
- comparing multiple creators;
- adding measurements;
- identifying test-condition differences;
- contextualizing a claim against previous models or competitors.

Display it as a separate layer:
**Creator observation → third-party interpretation → cross-source synthesis**

Never rewrite a third-party interpretation as though the creator said it.

## Full-review synthesis

After several complete reviews, answer:
- What stopped being important after real testing?
- What became more important?
- What do multiple reviewers independently agree on?
- Where do they disagree and why?
- Which claims still need longer-term testing?

Useful synthesis categories:
- imaging/control;
- performance/thermals;
- battery/charging;
- ergonomics/weight;
- software/ecosystem;
- value/upgrade context.

## Chinese creator ecosystem

For China-focused launches, search original Bilibili/Weibo content from relevant creators before relying on reposts. Creator selection should follow relevance and freshness, not a fixed mandatory list. Examples may include camera/tech channels, broad device reviewers, and specialist video creators.

## Avoid

- “都认为”“一致认为” without evidence.
- reducing a 20-minute full review to one marketing feature.
- treating a launch-floor hands-on as a long-term durability/battery conclusion.
- embedding a creator name merely for authority without describing their evidence.
